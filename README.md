# 🚀 Flagship Project

## 🧊 Igloo Asset Management System

### Full-Stack Web + Mobile Asset Management Platform

> An enterprise software platform designed to centralize commercial freezer asset operations, field activities, verification processes, and organizational workflows.

The **Igloo Asset Management System (AMS)** was developed during my Software Development internship at **Abdul Monem Ltd (Igloo)**.

The platform connects **administrative users, field teams, outlets, and commercial freezer assets** through a centralized backend shared by the web and Android applications.

### ✨ Key Capabilities

- 📦 Asset registration & lifecycle management
- 🏪 Outlet registration & asset assignment
- 🆔 Asset Identification Number (AIN) management
- 📱 QR-enabled asset identification & scanning
- ➕ New & existing asset onboarding
- ✅ Verification & approval workflows
- 🗺️ Verification mapping & history tracking
- 🔄 Rejection, correction & resubmission workflows
- 🚚 Asset transfer & return processes
- 🛠️ Repair & disposal workflows
- 📍 GPS-supported field check-ins
- 👥 User & access-right management
- 🔐 Role-based operational workflows

### 👥 Supported Roles

`System Admin` • `ASO` • `TSO` • `TSM` • `Technician`
